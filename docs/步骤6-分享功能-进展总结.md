## 🔧 关键技术实现

### 1. Share 模型查询优化
```python
# 查询被分享给我的文档
shares = Share.objects.filter(
    sharee=user,
    is_active=True,
    document__isnull=False
).select_related('document', 'sharer')

documents = [share.document for share in shares]
```

### 2. 序列化器扩展
```python

is_shared = BooleanField(read_only=True)  # 是否被分享
```
